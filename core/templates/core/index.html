FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What file formats are supported?
We support PDF and DOCX formats. Your document is processed securely and the extracted text is used for AI generation. We recommend clear, typed documents for best results.
Is it really free?
Yes! You get 5 AI generations per day on the free plan. No credit card required. Upgrade to Premium (₹199/month) for unlimited generations, uploads, and note downloads.
How accurate is the AI generation?
The AI reads your actual uploaded notes, so the generated content is based on your specific material. It uses Groq's LLM technology to create contextually relevant questions and summaries. Quality depends on the clarity of your source material.
Can I share my notes with others?
Yes! You can make your notes public so other students can discover them. You can also keep them private or share them with specific people. Public notes appear in the Browse section for the community.
How do I upgrade my plan?
Go to the Pricing page, choose Premium or Pro, and complete the payment via Razorpay. Premium gives you unlimited AI generations and note downloads. Pro includes all features plus priority support.
