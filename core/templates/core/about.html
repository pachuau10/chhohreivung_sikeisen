About Chhohreivung

AI-powered studying, made simple.

Our Mission

Chhohreivung helps students study smarter, not harder. Upload your notes and let AI generate MCQs, flashcards, summaries, and practice tests — all in seconds.

What We Offer
  • Upload PDF/DOCX notes and extract text
  • Generate multiple-choice, short, and long questions
  • Create digital flashcards with spaced repetition
  • AI-powered summaries of any study material
  • Timed practice tests with instant scoring
  • Community to share and discover study materials
Technology

Built with Django, PostgreSQL, and AI (Groq/Llama). Free to start with 5 AI generations daily.
